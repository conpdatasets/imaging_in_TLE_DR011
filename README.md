## Multi-modal Imaging Investigations of Individuals with Temporal Lobe Epilepsy and Excisions

# McAndrews M. P., Khan A., Peters T., Johnsrude I

# Overview

Clinical and imaging data on 118 patients with temporal lobe epilepsy (TLE), 72 who underwent excisions with post-operative outcomes recorded, are included from two epilepsy surgical centres in Ontario, Canada. Also included are MRI scans from 46 controls from the two scanners. These deeply phenotyped cases can be used to derive imaging correlates of TLE in relation to clinical features and surgical outcomes. MRI data from controls and limited EEG data are also available. 

# Data organization

Data is organized into packages by Treatment Group (Control vs Temporal Lobe Epilepsy), Timepoint, Imaging Scan Type, and Clinical Data Collection Domain.
